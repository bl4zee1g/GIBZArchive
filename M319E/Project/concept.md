### Mamdanistan breakout

the player finds themselves on times square in the middle of the night and has to find their way home. on their way home they see different people and have to decide whether they want to interact with them and help them. Some quests to help can be dangerous and lead to the player dying. Others are easy. If the player never chooses to help any of the people they comes across, they will also faill their quest to get home as one of the people they let down will be waiting in front of their home and kill the player.
